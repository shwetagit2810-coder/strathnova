Cancellation and refund.

Professional services, not products. Here is what happens if either of us stops.
Draft for legal reviewThese were prepared to a standard appropriate for an early-stage consulting LLP operating from India into North America and Europe. They are not legal advice and have not been reviewed by a lawyer. Items marked like this need a decision before publication.

1. What this covers

Strathnova sells senior professional services. There is no physical product, no download, and no subscription that renews automatically. This policy sets out what happens when an engagement is cancelled by you or by us.

2. Before any work begins

The first thirty-minute call is free and carries no obligation on either side. A scoped plan issued after that call is also free, and you are under no obligation to proceed.

If you cancel after signing but before work starts, any advance is refunded in full, less any third-party costs already committed on your instruction.

3. Fixed-price engagements

Startup in a Box and similar fixed-scope work is billed against milestones. If you cancel mid-engagement, you pay for milestones completed and accepted, plus work in progress on the current milestone. Anything paid beyond that is refunded.

Confirm the notice period: 14 days is typical.

4. Retainers

Strategic Consulting and Shadow Advisory are monthly. Either side may end the arrangement with 30 days written notice. The current month is payable in full; nothing beyond it is charged.

Unused hours in a month do not roll forward and are not refundable, because the retainer buys availability rather than a quantity of hours.

5. Build Operate Transfer

The build phase follows the fixed-price rules above. The operate phase follows the retainer rules. Transfer, once triggered, is completed and invoiced in full, because a partial handover leaves you with something you cannot run.

6. If we cancel

If we end an engagement for any reason other than your material breach, we refund all fees for work not yet delivered, and we hand over everything produced to that point in a usable state. We will also tell you who else could take it on.

This follows from a value rather than a policy: if we are not the right fit, we say so.

7. How to cancel

Write to your partner contact, or to a named billing address. We acknowledge within one business day and confirm the position in writing within five.

8. Refund timing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 14 working days.

If you take card or gateway payments, your provider is likely to require a published refund policy and may set its own timelines. Check their terms before publishing this.
